Nutrient Tracker

Results come from USDA FoodData Central. Pick a food to download its full nutrient breakdown (100+ nutrients for whole foods) into your library.

Averages only count days with something logged.

USDA API key

Searches use the free USDA FoodData Central API. Without a key the shared DEMO_KEY is used, which is limited to a few requests per hour. Get your own free key (takes a minute) at fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup.

Daily targets

Presets fill every target below; you can then fine-tune any value. Nutrients marked “limit” are upper limits — going over is flagged.
